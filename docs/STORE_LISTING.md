## Notes

- **Do not** put emoji or ALL-CAPS marketing shouting in the title or short
  description. Play's metadata policy treats that as spam and it is a common
  rejection reason.
- The tip jar is described as unlocking nothing, because it does not. Claiming
  otherwise would also contradict the App access answer ("no part of the app is
  restricted").
- Slovenian is spelled properly, diacritics and all. Play's listing editor
  handles them, and a Slovenian listing with the accents stripped out reads as
  carelessness to exactly the audience this is for.
- Notifications and the tip jar are both mentioned because both now work. Do
  not describe anything that does not - a listing promising a feature the app
  lacks is a metadata policy violation, not just a disappointment.
