## Full description (max 4000)

```
Someone just said something so unbelievably stupid that there was only one word
for it. Sejbosejbo.

Sejbosejbo is a small, deliberately chaotic archive of peak human nonsense. See
something absurd? Screenshot it, upload it, and let everyone decide whether it
truly qualifies.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Browse the archive. Every certified Sejbosejbo, newest first.
- Upload your own. A photo, a GIF, audio, video, or just a cursed story -
  whatever captures the moment. Paste straight from your clipboard if you have
  just taken a screenshot, and crop or rotate it before it goes up.
- Vote. Every post gets the only two verdicts that matter: SEJ BO, or SEJ NE BO.
- Argue about it. Every post has a comment thread, and comments get voted on
  too. No account needed for that either.
- Hall of Fame. The highest-scoring nonsense of all time, ranked.
- Daily award. One post is crowned every day.
- Get a notification when something new lands, if you want one. One per post,
  nothing else, and you can switch it off.
- Share anything to your group chat, where it belongs.
- Buy and sell stuff on Sejbo Market. Browse listings, message sellers and make
  offers. You settle the deal yourselves - no money moves through the app.

NO ADS, NO TRACKING

Browsing, posting, voting and commenting need no account and no login. There
are no ads, no analytics, and nothing following you around. You do not hand
over an email address to look at a picture of someone microwaving a salad.

The one exception is Sejbo Market: to sell or message someone, you log in with
a code sent to your email - no password. Nobody else ever sees your address,
and you can delete the account and everything in it from inside the app.

Posting is anonymous by design.

ENGLISH AND SLOVENIAN

Switch languages any time from the home screen. The whole app follows.

RUNS ON ONE OLD COMPUTER

No cloud, no data centre - just one small, old computer, quietly archiving
humanity's worst decisions. If you want to keep it awake, there is an
entirely optional tip jar. It unlocks nothing. Everything in the app is free,
forever, whether you tip or not.

Sejbosejbo is also on the web at sejbosejbo.fyi - the app and the site share the
same archive, so whatever you post shows up in both.
```

## Notes

- **Do not** put emoji or ALL-CAPS marketing shouting in the title or short
  description. Play's metadata policy treats that as spam and it is a common
  rejection reason.
- The tip jar is described as unlocking nothing, because it does not. Claiming
  otherwise would also contradict the App access answer ("no part of the app is
  restricted").
- Slovenian is spelled properly, diacritics and all. Play's listing editor
  handles them, and a Slovenian listing with the accents stripped out reads as
  carelessness to exactly the audience this is for.
- Notifications and the tip jar are both mentioned because both now work. Do
  not describe anything that does not - a listing promising a feature the app
  lacks is a metadata policy violation, not just a disappointment.
