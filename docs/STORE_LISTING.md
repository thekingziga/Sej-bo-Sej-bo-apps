Character limits are Play's own; every string below is within them.

## Full description (max 4000)

```
Someone just said something so unbelievably stupid that there was only one word
for it. Sejbosejbo.

Sejbosejbo is a small, deliberately chaotic archive of peak human nonsense. See
something absurd? Screenshot it, upload it, and let everyone decide whether it
truly qualifies.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Browse the archive. Every certified Sejbosejbo, newest first.
- Upload your own. A photo, a GIF, or just a cursed story - whatever captures
  the moment. Paste straight from your clipboard if you just took a screenshot.
- Vote. Every post gets the only two verdicts that matter: SEJ BO, or SEJ NE BO.
- Hall of Fame. The highest-scoring nonsense of all time, ranked.
- Daily award. One post is crowned every day.
- Share anything to your group chat, where it belongs.

NO ACCOUNTS, NO ADS, NO TRACKING

There is no sign-up. There is no login. There are no ads, no analytics, and
nothing following you around. You do not hand over an email address to look at
a picture of someone microwaving a salad.

Posting is anonymous by design.

ENGLISH AND SLOVENIAN

Switch languages any time from the home screen. The whole app follows.

RUNS ON A RASPBERRY PI

No cloud, no data centre - just a very small computer in a cupboard, quietly
archiving humanity's worst decisions. If you want to keep it awake, there is an
entirely optional tip jar. It unlocks nothing. Everything in the app is free,
forever, whether you tip or not.

Sejbosejbo is also on the web at sejbosejbo.fyi - the app and the site share the
same archive, so whatever you post shows up in both.
```

## Notes

- **Do not** put emoji or ALL-CAPS marketing shouting in the title or short
  description. Play's metadata policy treats that as spam and it is a common
  rejection reason.
- The tip jar is described as unlocking nothing, because it does not. Claiming
  otherwise would also contradict the App access answer ("no part of the app is
  restricted").
- Slovenian text avoids diacritics in a few places on purpose - Play's listing
  editor handles them fine, so restore c/s/z accents if you prefer; nothing
  depends on it.
